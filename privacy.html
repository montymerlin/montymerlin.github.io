Privacy policy

HQ agents (personal) · last updated 14 November 2026

Who uses it

HQ agents (personal) is a private tool used only by its author, on his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else's account is connected to it.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent through Google's sign-in, it reads and organises his Gmail (searching, reading, labelling, archiving and managing filters) and reads his Google Contacts and Calendar. It cannot send email.

Where the data goes

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. Data from Google is processed there and by the AI assistant he uses to work with it (Anthropic's Claude). Sign-in tokens are stored on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, after which the stored sign-in no longer works.
